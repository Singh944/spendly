Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and expense data including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect usage data to improve our service.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve our expense tracking service. This includes analyzing spending patterns, generating insights, and maintaining your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your financial information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Data is retained until you delete your account.

Third Party Services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to assist in operating our platform and processing data. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and may only access your data as necessary to perform their functions.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: privacy@spendly.com
